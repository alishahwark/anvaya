# UX and content review

The primary repair focus was integration clarity rather than a visual redesign. The Evidence destination is now present in both desktop and mobile homepage navigation. The Evidence Lab explains its CPI unit, base year, geography, descriptive scope, and causal limitation next to the chart. Policy records visibly separate lifecycle status from evidence confidence, and unverified records retain explicit unavailable-source language.

The remaining content debt is deliberate and documented: the seeded empty records have no authenticated source to summarize, and AI-generated stakeholder/explanation content is unavailable until the approved key is configured. Those states are shown as unavailable rather than replaced with generic policy claims.
