# User test protocol (15 minutes per participant)

**Status: not yet run.** The report states that no user study was carried out. Run this with 3–5 people who are not lawyers or developers, record what happens, and add the results to the report's Impact section. If you cannot recruit anyone, leave the report as it is: it is honest.

## Setup
Open the home page on a laptop. Do not explain the product. Say only: "Please think aloud."

## Test 1 — five seconds
Show the home page for five seconds, then hide it. Ask:
1. What is this for?
2. What would you do next?

Pass: they say something like "to understand laws or policies" and "search / ask a question".

## Test 2 — two minutes
Give them this task, with no help: *"You run a small online shop. Find out whether the DPDP Act affects you and by when."* Start a timer. Stop at two minutes.

Ask afterwards (write the exact answers):
- (a) What is the DPDP Act? (b) Is it in force? (c) What changes? (d) Does it affect you? (e) Why should you trust this?
- Which one thing did you find, and where did you click?
- Did you open any evidence or a PDF? Was it clear what it showed?

Pass per question: the answer matches the page (a, b, c, d) and they can name a reason to trust it, such as "it shows the original passage" (e).
